Privacy Policy

Floru Studio · Last updated: October 17, 2026

Floru Studio ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal tool built and operated by an individual developer, Linh Hoang. It helps the developer manage YouTube channels that the developer owns. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, stored, and shared, and how you can revoke access or request deletion.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services, including the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access and collect

When you authorize the App with your Google account through Google OAuth, the App may access the following, depending on the permissions you grant:

DataPurpose
OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by GoogleCalling YouTube APIs on your behalf without signing in each time
Channel information (channel ID, title, avatar, banner, subscriber count)Identifying the connected channel and showing it in the App
Video list and video details, including unlisted and private videos (title, description, thumbnail, privacy status, view/like/comment counts)Showing the channel's video library and status
Video metadata you create in the App (title, description, tags, category, privacy status, scheduled time)Uploading and publishing videos
YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscriber changes)Tracking channel performance and monetization progress
Comments on the channel's videosReading and replying to comments from within the App

The App does not access your Google password, payment information, contacts, email, or any data unrelated to the YouTube functions described above.

3. How we use the information

  • To upload videos and manage their metadata and publishing schedule on the authorized channel.
  • To display channel, video, analytics, and comment information inside the App.
  • To generate improvement suggestions for the channel based on its aggregated statistics.

The information is used only to provide these features. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold.

4. Storage and retention

  • Data is stored in the App's private database on infrastructure controlled by the developer. OAuth tokens are stored securely and are never exposed publicly.
  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is not kept longer than necessary. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • When you revoke access or request deletion, the related tokens and stored YouTube data are deleted within 7 days.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent your information. Data is sent to Google/YouTube through the official APIs to perform the actions you request.

To generate channel suggestions, aggregated channel and video statistics (such as view counts and watch time) and video titles may be sent to a third-party AI service provider for processing. OAuth tokens, account credentials, and personal contact information are never shared. Such providers process the data only to return the result and do not receive access to your YouTube account.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Cookies and device information

The App does not place cookies or similar tracking technologies on your device, and does not collect device identifiers for tracking. This website does not use cookies or analytics.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time through the Google security settings page. After revocation, the App can no longer access your YouTube data.

8. Requesting deletion

To request deletion of any data the App has stored, contact us at the email below. Requests are handled within 7 days.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
