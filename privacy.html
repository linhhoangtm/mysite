Privacy Policy

Gemi YT Creator · Last updated: October 17, 2026

Gemi YT Creator ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal tool built and operated by an individual developer, Linh Hoang. It helps the developer manage and schedule uploads of video content to YouTube channels that the developer owns. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, stored, and shared, and how you can revoke access or request deletion.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and manage video metadata on YouTube. By using the App, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access and collect

When you authorize the App with your Google account through Google OAuth, the App may access:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google, used to call the YouTube Data API on your behalf.
  • Basic channel information such as channel ID, channel title, and thumbnail, used to identify which channel content is uploaded to.
  • Video metadata you create in the App, such as titles, descriptions, tags, category, privacy status, and scheduled publish time.
  • Upload results returned by the API, such as video IDs and processing status.

The App does not access your Google password, payment information, contacts, email, or any data unrelated to the YouTube functions described above.

3. How we use the information

  • To upload videos and set their metadata on the authorized YouTube channel.
  • To schedule publishing and display upload status inside the App.
  • To keep the connection to the channel active without re-authorizing every time.

The information is used only to provide these features. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sold to anyone.

4. Storage and retention

  • Data is stored in the App's private database on infrastructure controlled by the developer. OAuth tokens are stored securely and are never exposed publicly.
  • Data obtained from YouTube API Services is not kept longer than necessary. Stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • When you revoke access or request deletion, the related tokens and stored YouTube data are deleted within 7 days.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube through the official APIs in order to perform the actions you request. We may disclose information only if required by law.

6. Cookies and device information

The App does not place cookies or similar tracking technologies on your device, and does not collect device identifiers for tracking. This website itself does not use cookies or analytics.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time through the Google security settings page. After revocation, the App can no longer access your YouTube data.

8. Requesting deletion

To request deletion of any data the App has stored, contact us at the email below. Requests are handled within 7 days.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
