Floru Studio

A personal desktop tool for managing the developer's own YouTube channels: preparing and scheduling uploads, tracking channel performance, and keeping up with comments.

About

Floru Studio is built and operated by an individual developer, Linh Hoang. It is used internally to manage YouTube channels owned by the developer. It is not a public service, and no third-party users connect their accounts to it.

Features

Video upload

Uploads finished videos to the developer's channel through the official YouTube Data API.

Metadata & scheduling

Sets title, description, tags, category, privacy status, and planned publish time.

Channel overview

Lists the channel's videos, including unlisted and private ones, with their status and basic stats.

Performance tracking

Shows subscribers, views, and watch time from YouTube Analytics to follow channel growth.

Comments

Displays comments on the developer's videos so they can be read and answered in one place.

Suggestions

Generates improvement suggestions for the channel based on its aggregated statistics.

How it uses YouTube

  1. The channel owner signs in with Google OAuth and grants the requested permissions.
  2. The App uploads videos, manages metadata, and reads channel data using YouTube API Services.
  3. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.
