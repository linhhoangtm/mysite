Gemi YT Creator

A personal tool for preparing, scheduling, and uploading videos to YouTube channels owned by the developer.

About

Gemi YT Creator is built and operated by an individual developer, Linh Hoang. It is used internally to manage the developer's own YouTube channels. It is not a public service, and no third-party users connect their accounts to it.

Features

Video upload

Uploads finished videos to the developer's channel through the official YouTube Data API.

Metadata management

Sets title, description, tags, category, and privacy status for each video.

Scheduling

Schedules publish times so content goes live at a planned moment.

Status tracking

Shows upload and processing status returned by YouTube.

How it uses YouTube

  1. The channel owner signs in with Google OAuth and grants upload permission.
  2. The App uploads videos and sets their metadata using YouTube API Services.
  3. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.
